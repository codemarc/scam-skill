Ask the country only when local recovery or reporting advice depends on it. Prefer the appropriate local regulator outside the US; do not assume US guidance applies everywhere.

A search result is a lead. Read the underlying official page before claiming it supports a finding. Report source dates and exact entity matches where these affect the conclusion. Do not present a general enforcement case as an action against the submitted domain.
